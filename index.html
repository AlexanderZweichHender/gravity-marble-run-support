Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap where you want to aim and the launcher shoots a marble into the chain. Match three or more marbles of the same color to clear them. Clear the chain before it reaches the end of the track.

My progress disappeared. What can I do?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it. If you restored your device from an iCloud or computer backup, your progress may be restored with it.

The game crashes or freezes.

Make sure you have the latest version of the game from the App Store and restart your device. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.

Is the game free?

Yes, Gravity Marble Run is free to download and play.
